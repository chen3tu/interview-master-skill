**Interviewer:** Tell me how you would evaluate whether your onboarding checklist helped users activate and continue using the product.

**候选人（提供的回复）：**“暂停，直接教我怎么回答。”

已暂停模拟。这个问题要把三件事说清楚：你做了什么、目前能证明什么、以后会怎样验证效果。你协调了清单及与支持团队的每周问题回顾，平台改动由工程团队交付；现有信息不能支持具体提升幅度，也不能直接支持因果结论。

英文示范答案：

“I coordinated the onboarding checklist and weekly issue reviews with support, while engineering delivered the platform changes. That describes my contribution, but I cannot currently verify the conversion figure in my résumé or isolate the effect of my work. To evaluate the impact, I would first define activation for an eligible signup cohort and check the event data and measurement window. If feasible, I would compare randomly assigned workspaces receiving the change with a control group, then examine retention and support issues as well as activation. I would only recommend expansion if the evidence supported useful, sustained improvement without unacceptable side effects.”

前两句讲已知经历与证据边界，后面用 “I would” 讲方法，没有把未做过的实验写成经历。若不能随机分组，可以考虑可比同期群，但要解释选择偏差，不能把前后对比自动当成因果证据。

当前停在“如何评价 onboarding 效果”这一题。你还没有作答，所以没有专业能力评分；这份示范也不算你的独立表现。下一步可以先用自己的话复述“已知贡献”和“验证方法”两部分。
