你的故事库目前只有 onboarding 一段经历：它可以覆盖贡献、协作和数据反思，但不是三个独立项目。激活分析与留存评估可以用方法回答，不能包装成已经做过。

**第 1 题：Tell me about an onboarding project and your contribution.**

考察：经历真实性、个人边界、岗位相关性。优先级最高；已有经历能支持协调工作，但具体决策和成果数据不足。建议口述 60–90 秒。

> “The most relevant example is an onboarding initiative where I coordinated a checklist and weekly issue reviews with support. Engineering delivered the platform changes, so my contribution was the coordination work rather than the technical implementation or ownership of the entire product delivery.
>
> “This is relevant to the role because onboarding connects the user’s early experience with cross-functional execution. However, I want to be careful about the outcome claim: my résumé says conversion improved by 20%, but I cannot currently verify the denominator, the comparison period, or whether that was a relative change. I should not present that number as a confirmed result or claim that my work caused it.
>
> “If I were evaluating the initiative now, I would first reconstruct the conversion definition and compare equivalent user cohorts, taking the engineering changes into account. I would also examine whether users who activated continued to receive value and return. The main lesson I would apply in this role is to define ownership and measurement before rollout, so we can explain both what we delivered and what the evidence actually supports.”

三种追问：

- **“What exactly did you own?”** “I coordinated the onboarding checklist and weekly issue reviews with support. Engineering delivered the platform changes.”
- **危险追问：“Where did 20% come from?”** “I cannot verify its basis from the information I currently have. I should have checked it before including it, and I am correcting that claim.”
- **迁移追问：“What would you do differently here?”** “I would agree on the activation event, eligible cohort, comparison design, and ownership before rollout, then evaluate retention as well as immediate conversion.”

注意：不要为了让故事完整，补出未经提供的用户痛点、评审争论、上线时间或结果。

**第 2 题：How would you coordinate delivery across support and engineering without managing them?**

考察：横向协作、取舍、交付意识。现有事实支持你参与协调，不支持你管理团队。建议口述 60–90 秒。

> “I have not managed people. My relevant experience is coordinating an onboarding checklist and weekly issue reviews with support, while engineering delivered the platform changes. I would use that experience as a starting point for cross-functional coordination, without overstating my authority.
>
> “For a new initiative, I would first align the teams on the user problem, the outcome we want, and the constraints. I would separate issues that support can address through guidance from issues that require a product change. For engineering work, I would clarify the problem and acceptance criteria with the team, ask about dependencies and effort, and agree on explicit owners and next steps.
>
> “If priorities conflicted, I would compare user impact, urgency, evidence, and implementation cost. A smaller change or a temporary support process might be a better first step than a broad platform change. I would use regular reviews to surface blockers and decisions, rather than treating meetings as evidence of progress. My goal would be clear decisions and accountable follow-through, while leaving implementation choices with engineering and escalating unresolved priority decisions to the appropriate owner.”

这段从第二段开始是拟采用的方法，不是在补造过往行动。

三种追问：

- **“Did you decide engineering priorities in that project?”** “The information I can substantiate is my coordination role. I should not claim final prioritization authority.”
- **“What if engineering says no?”** “I would understand the constraint, revisit scope and alternatives, and make the impact of deferring the work explicit before asking the accountable decision-maker to resolve the trade-off.”
- **迁移追问：“How would you know this process works here?”** “I would check whether decisions and blockers are resolved, whether agreed work reaches acceptance, and whether the user outcome improves. I would not judge it by meeting frequency alone.”

**第 3 题：How would you evaluate whether an onboarding change improves activation and retention?**

考察：指标定义、因果判断、长期价值。输入没有证明你做过实验或留存分析；用假设方法回答。建议口述 90 秒。

> “I would define success before choosing a solution. First, I would agree on an activation event that reflects meaningful user value, the eligible user population, and a fixed time window. Activation would be the share of eligible users who complete that event within the window. I would define retention separately using a stable cohort and a meaningful return activity after an appropriate period.
>
> “Next, I would validate the tracking and choose a comparison design. Where feasible, I would randomly assign eligible users or accounts before exposure, avoid contamination between groups, and keep the measurement windows consistent. I would monitor both activation and retention, along with relevant guardrails such as support burden or errors. I would wait for the retention cohorts to mature rather than comparing users with different follow-up lengths.
>
> “If activation improved but retention did not, I would investigate whether we had made a shallow action easier without increasing lasting value, or whether the result differed by user segment. If randomization were unavailable, I would use the strongest credible comparison available and explain its limitations. A before-and-after increase alone would not establish causality. That is also why I would not rely on the unverified 20% figure from my previous project.”

三种追问：

- **“What is the denominator?”** “The eligible cohort defined before measurement, not just the users who completed onboarding.”
- **“How would you report a change from 10% to 12%?”** “That would be a 2-percentage-point increase, or a 20% relative increase.” 这是演算示例，不是你的项目数据。
- **迁移追问：“Would you always run an A/B test?”** “No. I would consider feasibility, interference, and risk. Without a credible experiment, I would use an appropriate alternative comparison and limit the strength of my causal claim.”

最后 5 分钟记住：数据口径与因果归因是高风险点；个人职责边界已能明确；具体优先级案例、实验经验、留存结果仍未提供。不要为了凑数字编造两个成果数据。

可以反问面试官：“How does your team define activation, and how do you evaluate whether activation improvements translate into retention?” 这句话直接对应 JD，也能帮你确认该岗位实际要解决的问题。
