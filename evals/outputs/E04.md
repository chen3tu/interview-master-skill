The method for recovering and validating the claim is:

1. Find the original report or calculation. Define the conversion event, eligible population, numerator, denominator, unit and observation window.
2. Reconstruct baseline and comparison periods. Check deduplication, eligibility changes, cohort maturity and instrumentation changes.
3. Calculate absolute and relative changes separately. Hypothetically, 15% to 18% is +3 percentage points or +20% relative; those example numbers are not your result.
4. Identify a credible comparison. Randomized assignment may support causal estimation if valid; a before/after comparison alone cannot separate your work from channel mix, seasonality or concurrent platform changes.
5. Examine retention and guardrails separately. More conversions do not automatically mean lasting value, and a team-level effect does not isolate your individual contribution.

If records remain unavailable, the result stays unknown. You can still prepare a strong explanation of the known actions and a hypothetical evaluation plan. The next useful step is recovering the source calculation, not inventing a softer number.
