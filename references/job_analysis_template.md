# JD analysis output

Use [JD-to-question method](jd_question_design.md). Translate headings into the chosen artifact language.

## One-page role judgment
Target/function/subdirection; level inferred from responsibility; business context; confirmed round; essential outcomes; evidence-backed strengths; consequential unknowns and next action.

## Mapping
| JD wording/source | Work outcome | Capability/level | Candidate evidence ID | Gap type | Verification question | Preparation action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Gap types: not presented, unknown, explicitly lacking experience, weak observed performance. Keep them distinct. If no candidate information exists, complete the task/capability side and leave personal judgment unavailable.

## Priorities
Explain why each priority matters and what would change it. Separate explicit requirements from inferred emphasis. Do not equate word order, job title or years with a precise employer scoring weight.

## Company/round notes
Only include researched facts that affect the preparation. Mark source/date and community experience vs official information vs inference. Give recruiter questions for consequential unknowns.
