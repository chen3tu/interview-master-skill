# JD analysis output

Use [JD-to-question method](jd_question_design.md). Translate headings into the chosen artifact language.

## Fit gate before preparation

Run this before the mapping. It decides how much preparation is worth producing, not whether the analysis happens.

- **Eligibility.** A stated requirement the candidate cannot meet at all: a licence, a degree, a years-of-experience floor, a work-authorisation class. Hard stop for a complete packet. Quote the wording, name the requirement, and offer targeted practice instead.
- **Language.** A required working language the candidate's materials do not evidence is a question, not a verdict: "not evidenced" is not "cannot". Ask before drafting in it, and skip a complete packet only if they confirm they do not work in that language. Where they do work in it but the posting's stated bar reads higher, prepare and show both wordings.
- **Practical constraints.** Location, start date or a stated compensation band that conflicts with the user's non-negotiables. State the conflict; do not resolve it for them.
- **Role level.** The posting's level and function against where the candidate is now and where they are going: a title or scope that reads as a step down, or a function that leaves their main line (a product manager returning to pure operations). Flag, never a hard stop. Say what the step would and would not preserve, and let the user decide.

Silence is not permission. A posting that omits a requirement has not waived it; mark it unverified rather than passing it. A company-wide statement that it welcomes a group of applicants is not role-level permission.

Report the gate as one line before anything else, so it is checkable:
- `Gate: pass` - every check clear, and the posting states what was checked.
- `Gate: pass with unverified: <items>` - nothing blocks, but the posting is silent on those items.
- `Gate: flag - <which check, quoted wording>` - proceed, with the finding visible.
- `Gate: hard stop - <which check, quoted wording>` - do not produce a complete packet unless asked.

The last two lines may carry `; unverified: <items>` for checks the posting left open, so one line holds both a finding and a silence.

Put it at the top of the role judgment so the rest is read in its light. If the user still wants a complete packet after a hard stop, produce it and keep the finding visible instead of deleting it.

## One-page role judgment
Target/function/subdirection; level inferred from responsibility; business context; confirmed round; essential outcomes; evidence-backed strengths; consequential unknowns and next action.

## Mapping
| JD wording/source | Work outcome | Capability/level | Candidate evidence ID | Gap type | Verification question | Preparation action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Gap types: not presented, unknown, explicitly lacking experience, weak observed performance. Keep them distinct. If no candidate information exists, complete the task/capability side and leave personal judgment unavailable.

## Priorities
Explain why each priority matters and what would change it. Separate explicit requirements from inferred emphasis. Do not equate word order, job title or years with a precise employer scoring weight.

## Company/round notes
Only include researched facts that affect the preparation. Mark source/date and community experience vs official information vs inference. Give recruiter questions for consequential unknowns.
