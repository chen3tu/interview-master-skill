# Early career and transitions

For campus/trainee candidates use projects, study, internships, volunteering or other actual evidence with honest scope. Assess understanding, structured reasoning, learning and collaboration; do not demand production ownership or people management.

For a career transition map:
previous task → transferable mechanism → target task → difference/limit → new work sample.
A similar metric does not make two roles identical. Show a gap explicitly and a realistic practice path.

Question adaptation:
- Junior: explain one decision, what you checked and what you learned.
- Independent: explain ownership, alternatives, delivery and validation.
- Senior: explain ambiguity, wider consequences, systemic mechanisms and tradeoffs.

Years, school, GPA, employer brand and accent do not substitute for task evidence. Ask about background only when relevant to a real requirement or story. Do not treat gaps or a career change as automatic weakness. Preserve credentials and titles when translating; do not imply foreign equivalence without support.
