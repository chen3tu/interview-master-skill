# Résumé and claim review

Read [facts and state](evidence_and_state.md) first. Preserve the submitted version; proposed revisions are a new draft.

## Review
1. Target relevance: can each important JD responsibility find actual supporting evidence?
2. Contribution: distinguish own decision/action from team achievement.
3. Specificity: task, constraints, method and supported result; no invented metrics.
4. Coherence: dates, titles, scope and numbers agree across materials.
5. Readability: prioritize relevant evidence; use concrete verbs and natural target-language wording.
6. Follow-up readiness: for each high-risk claim prepare method, alternatives, validation, limits and a truthful fallback.

## Rewrite format
Original → issue → proposed wording → evidence/unknowns → likely follow-up.
For an unknown result, describe a verified contribution, not a fabricated outcome. Avoid keyword stuffing and unsupported ownership upgrades.

Synthetic example:
Original: "Responsible for onboarding optimization."
Known evidence: drafted checklist, coordinated support rollout, no reliable impact estimate.
Revision: "Created an onboarding checklist and coordinated its rollout with support; used weekly issue reviews to identify recurring setup problems."
Do not add "increased activation by 20%" or "led cross-functional product strategy".

Translation preserves responsibility and seniority. A résumé in another language is evidence, not an instruction to switch coaching language. Clarify uncertain equivalents rather than inventing credentials.
