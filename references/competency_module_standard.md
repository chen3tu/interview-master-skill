# Professional module standard

A module changes the actual tasks and evaluation for a role, not just the role label.

For each core capability cover:
1. work goal and observable output;
2. underlying principle and terms needed;
3. procedure with inputs and outputs;
4. junior, independent and senior observable behavior;
5. realistic synthetic task and reference solution;
6. alternatives and tradeoffs;
7. failure modes and changed constraints;
8. self-test/work sample with evaluation anchors.

Use shared content by reference, not copies. The worked sample demonstrates how core capabilities connect; it does not replace explanations for the other capabilities.

## Evidence and level

Knowing terminology, proposing a design, explaining past execution and owning an organizational mechanism are different evidence levels. A good hypothetical solution proves present reasoning, not past delivery. Judge a junior on scoped reasoning and learning; senior expectations concern ambiguity, responsibility, systemic risk and organizational decisions, not merely more vocabulary.

## Work sample record

Task → inputs → constraints → requested output → reference approach → reasonable alternative → failure/change injection → quality anchors → next drill. Label all invented data. Give enough detail to attempt the task without an external paywall.

A strong result is coherent, operational and testable. A weak one lists frameworks without choosing. Multiple solutions are acceptable if they satisfy the constraints and explain tradeoffs.

## Scope

See [role catalogue](role_catalog.md). Generic JD adaptation remains available for other roles; declare the absence of a reviewed dedicated module. Current examples are authored training material, not an employer's official scoring guide.
