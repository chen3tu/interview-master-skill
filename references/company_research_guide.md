# Company and interview research

Research should change a preparation decision. Use available browsing tools; the skill does not require a particular vendor/tool name or logged-in service.

## Scope by task
Sprint: product/customer, role context, a meaningful recent development if verifiable, and interview process if available.
Complete/deep: add business model, strategy, team dependencies, competitors, user feedback and risks where useful.
A company name does not mandate seven exhaustive sections. Directly answer a narrow knowledge or salary question using only needed research.

## Sources
Prefer official company/job/product material for stated facts, primary technical documentation for technical claims, and clearly attributed reports for external interpretation. Community interview accounts are self-reports, not official question banks.

For a material claim record source URL/title, publication or retrieval date, role/region/round if applicable, type (fact/report/inference), confidence limit and what it changes. Read source content when possible. A search snippet remains a snippet, not verified full evidence.

Search using company/product and local-language terms, role synonyms, interview/面试/面经/mianshi as appropriate. Use current relevant dates, not hard-coded past years. Match target market independently from conversation language. Do not assume English means a US employer.

## Interview questions and uncertainty
Label "community-reported question", "official sample" or "original practice scenario". Preserve context and age. If reports are sparse, generate JD-based practice explicitly; do not invent reports to reach a requested number or transfer another company's questions without attribution.

Contradictory sources: show the material conflict and avoid certainty. Missing browsing/access: state the limit and continue from supplied JD. Never invent URLs, source quotes, current salaries or private hiring rubrics.

## Research output
Fact → implication for preparation → affected answer/question → remaining uncertainty.
A list of links without this connection is not useful preparation. Social likes and titles alone cannot establish user sentiment or team culture.
