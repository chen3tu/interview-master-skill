# Facts, evidence and session state

Keep three layers: candidate history; target-specific preparation; actual training observations. Only record fields that affect decisions.

## Fact record

Use stable IDs such as F01. Record source/exact wording, status, relevant project, date or version when known, and any unresolved conflict. Metrics need relevant units, period, baseline and ownership when available, not an exhaustive questionnaire.

| State | Meaning | Allowed use |
|---|---|---|
| confirmed | Candidate explicitly confirmed, or supplied primary material supports the exact claim | Historical answer within its actual scope; label source, not "independently audited" |
| résumé claim | Appears in résumé but not clarified | Attribute to the résumé; do not amplify responsibility or precision |
| inference | A reasoned interpretation | State uncertainty and alternatives; never silently promote |
| hypothetical | Invented for teaching or practice | Separate example only, never first-person biography |
| unknown | Not provided or cannot be recalled | Omit detail, use a known action, or teach a method |
| conflict | Sources disagree | Keep both sources, withhold disputed detail and request clarification |

A previous AI draft is not confirmation. Translation, repetition or polished formatting never changes status. "I don't remember" stays unknown; do not substitute an unsupported range, "double-digit improvement", or a confident qualitative outcome.

## Update procedure

1. Reuse available material, preserve submitted résumé version.
2. Identify the smallest facts affecting target fit, main claims or answer credibility.
3. Draft using supported details; keep uncertain numbers out of the spoken historical claim.
4. Batch the consequential questions after useful work.
5. On clarification, record source/change and update linked answers only. Preserve user edits; propose conflicting edits visibly rather than overwriting.

A résumé with no evidence for a skill means "not evidenced", not "incapable". Distinguish absent presentation, unknown experience and an explicitly stated gap. A hypothetical work sample can demonstrate present reasoning but cannot prove past employment experience.

## Minimal session summary

- target_id; JD/source/version; submitted résumé version
- mode, output_format, depth, pressure, time_budget
- coaching_language, answer_language, mock_language, target_market, register
- relevant facts with IDs/states; story IDs and contribution boundaries
- question IDs, evidence links, coverage and actual response observations
- delivered artifacts/sections; user edits; unresolved items; current question; next action

Optional workspace: profile.md, facts.md, stories.md and targets/company-role/{jd,research,prep,questions,sessions}. Adapt existing locations; keep all candidate data outside the skill and release tree. Do not claim persistent memory unless it exists. Without files provide this summary in chat.

Cross-language versions share fact IDs. Never convert currency, percent to percentage points, dates, grades or titles without an explicit, supported transformation. Version a translated answer independently from the factual source.

## Historical answer vs learning

Historical: "I owned the channel rollout and monitored weekly retention. I cannot confirm the exact lift."
Method: explain cohort definition, comparison and confounders.
Decision: explain what evidence would justify expansion or rollback.
A separate hypothetical example may include invented values, explicitly labelled before the example. Do not interpolate those values back into the candidate's answer.
