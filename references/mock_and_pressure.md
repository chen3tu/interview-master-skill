# Adaptive mock interview

## Setup
Use known target, round, time/question budget and [language settings](language_and_locale.md). If a missing setting is not decisive, state a sensible assumption and start. Standard pressure is default; the user's request for challenge/high pressure authorizes it without repeated confirmation.

Keep a private working plan of capabilities to sample, not a rigid transcript. Tell the user the broad scope, not the answer key. Ask one relevant question and wait.

## Branch on the actual answer
- Vague: ask for a specific action, input, decision or outcome.
- Unsupported metric: clarify baseline, denominator, comparison and ownership. If unknown, record it; do not endlessly demand recall.
- Strong: introduce a plausible constraint, alternative or failure.
- Contradictory: show the conflict neutrally and ask for reconciliation.
- Unfamiliar topic: distinguish lack of knowledge from reasoning; ask how they would investigate if relevant.
- Sufficient evidence: change topic to improve coverage; do not keep attacking a supported claim.

Pressure levels:
standard: normal clarification and tradeoffs;
challenge: alternative explanations, contribution boundaries and rejected options;
high: competing goals, reduced resources, tighter requested answer length and sequential follow-ups.
No insults, stereotypes, arbitrary disbelief or compulsory background interrogation. The user can pause immediately. Pressure does not make the same answer less competent.

## Pause and transition
"Pause", "explain", "show the answer", or equivalents in the user's language end the current simulated exchange. Respond as coach in the coaching language, keep the pending question and observations, and offer the requested explanation. A restart after seeing an answer is assisted practice, not independent assessment.

In formal mock, defer grammar coaching to the debrief unless requested otherwise. Keep interviewer language stable across turns. For text-only practice use agreed length or user timing; do not claim audio measurements.

## Debrief
Use [evaluation](interview_evaluation_criteria.md). Quote representative actual responses, identify coverage, distinguish knowledge/evidence/expression/judgment gaps and provide improved answers plus a next drill. A mock cannot establish actual hiring probability. User-provided actual interview recollections are not independently verified transcripts.
