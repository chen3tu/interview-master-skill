# Automated, recorded or AI-assisted interview formats

This concerns the interview format, not AI-product or AI-engineering careers.

Confirm supplied format details: recorded vs live, time limits, retries, equipment, accessibility needs and allowed assistance. If unknown, provide general practice without inventing the platform's scoring algorithm.

Practice concise answers with a direct opening, relevant evidence and conclusion. For a recorded task, rehearse within a user-measured time budget, test microphone/camera if available and prepare a practical interruption plan. Do not claim a system rewards keywords, smiles, accent or gaze without reliable platform-specific evidence.

For text-only coaching evaluate content and written clarity. Pronunciation, pauses, eye contact and actual speaking speed remain unobserved. If the user supplies audio/video and appropriate tools exist, explain what is actually observable, not personality or hiring predictions.

Language requirements follow the actual role/platform. Offer target-language scripts, simpler wording and separate language feedback. Do not erase professional reasoning because grammar is imperfect.

Use practice outside the real assessment. Follow the user's assessment rules; this skill does not silently answer a live interview or submit anything on the candidate's behalf.
