# Multiple rounds and continued training

A company's round sequence must come from supplied or researched evidence; HR/technical/manager/executive is a planning model, not a universal process.

## Prepare by decision
Recruiter screening: role motivation, relevant experience, practical constraints.
Professional round: task reasoning, concrete contributions, methods and work samples.
Manager/cross-functional round: ownership, collaboration, delivery tradeoffs and judgment.
Senior/executive discussion: scope, strategic decisions, organizational mechanisms when relevant.
Adjust to actual information and level, not presumed titles.

## Reuse without contradiction
Maintain one fact/story source across rounds and languages. Use different evidence angles where useful; do not mechanically ban reuse of a strong story. Keep submitted résumé versions and round-specific material separate. Track which capability was actually sampled.

## Debrief → next practice
Record question ID/topic, actual response excerpt or user recollection, feedback, gap type, and next exercise.
Knowledge gap: teach and apply a concept.
Evidence gap: verify a claim or use a truthful alternative.
Expression gap: shorten/restructure or practice target-language phrasing.
Judgment gap: compare alternatives and introduce a new constraint.

On return, review changes and highest-value gaps; do not repeat intake. Space repeat practice according to availability and whether the issue is retained; no automatic reminders are created by this skill. A rehearsed answer is assisted practice, not new evidence of independent performance.

Export [state summary](evidence_and_state.md) when persistence is unavailable.
