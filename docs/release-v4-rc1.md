# Interview Master v4.0.0-rc.1 — release text draft

Status: local candidate; not published. Live Claude installation remains open.

Interview preparation can now start in sprint, complete, coaching or mock mode. Complete preparation produces usable material before batching missing facts; mock interviews remain sequential and can pause into teaching. Evidence states preserve contribution boundaries and unresolved claims across sessions.

Coaching, model answers and mock dialogue can use different requested languages. Chinese/English, Spanish/French samples and mid-session switches have been exercised, with factual fidelity checks. Hiring market is independent of language; language fluency and professional judgment receive separate feedback.

Role modules include product, operations/growth, data, technical, AI product and management, with backend, frontend, mobile and AI application engineering specializations. Worked examples teach methods, alternatives, failure conditions and role levels. Direct offer, salary, reverse-question and debrief routes remain available.

Validation: 17 deterministic tests; 36 behavioral categories, eight v3 baseline cases, six paired role cases and two fresh extracted-package scenarios reviewed. This does not establish live-host compatibility, native-level quality in every language or statistically proven improvement over v3.

Candidate archive: interview-master-v4.0.0-rc.1.zip. Keep the full interview-master folder and references. Back up the previous installation; rollback replaces only the skill package, preserving candidate materials.
