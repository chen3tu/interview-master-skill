# Source notes

The v4 design was informed by static review of 21 public repositories across interview skills, applications, architecture, technical knowledge, Chinese interview accounts and product/management resources. This is not evidence of their runtime effectiveness.

Mechanisms independently implemented here include configurable preparation, evidence-to-question mapping, worked role tasks, separate coverage/quality evaluation and bounded pressure. All shipped work samples are newly written synthetic scenarios.

Representative mechanism references:
- [Interview coach](https://github.com/noamseg/interview-coach-skill): state and evidence/story mapping.
- [Backend simulator](https://github.com/Hazehacker/backend-interview-simulator): evidence and observation coverage.
- [CareerKit](https://github.com/Chozzc/Lujie-Careerkit): explicit preparation deliverables.
- [System design primer](https://github.com/donnemartin/system-design-primer): knowledge-to-practice organization.
- [JavaGuide](https://github.com/Snailclimb/JavaGuide): project decisions and verification.
- [Product interview preparation](https://github.com/lan1177/interview-prep): direct module requests and time-sensitive preparation.

No third-party source text or diagrams are copied into the runtime. In particular, get-job.skill and system-design-101 carry restrictive CC BY-NC-ND terms, mobile-system-design reserves rights, and several reviewed repositories have no identified license. Their public visibility is not a reuse grant. Linked books/PDFs retain their own rights.

Personal coaching experience informed the general fact/method/decision structure, but no real candidate biography, company-specific preparation or personal metric is included.

Installation documentation was checked against [Claude's official guide](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12512180-use-skills-in-claude) on 2026-10-20; this does not imply live host validation.
